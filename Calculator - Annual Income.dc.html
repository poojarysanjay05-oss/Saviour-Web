How this is calculated

Whatever pay period you're given, this tool scales it up to a full year using standard conventions: 52 weeks, 260 working days, or 12 months.

Annual = Hourly Rate × Hours/Week × 52

Example: ₹500/hour at 40 hours/week comes to ₹10,40,000 a year.

Tips for comparing offers

  • Always compare annualised numbers, not raw hourly or monthly figures.
  • Factor in benefits (PF, insurance, bonus) missing from a freelance rate.
  • Account for unpaid leave or slow months if your work isn't steady year-round.
  • Use take-home, not gross, when comparing your real spending power.
