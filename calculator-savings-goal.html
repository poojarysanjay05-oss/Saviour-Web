How this is calculated

Each month, your existing balance earns interest and you add a fresh contribution. This calculator simulates that growth month by month until your balance reaches your target.

Balancen = Balancen-1 × (1 + r) + Monthly Savings

Example: saving ₹10,000/month at 7% annual return reaches a ₹5,00,000 goal in about 45 months — roughly 4 months sooner than with no interest at all.

Tips to reach your goal faster

  • Automate the monthly transfer so saving isn't a decision you re-make every month.
  • Put any bonus or refund directly toward the goal instead of spending it.
  • Choose an account or fund that matches your timeline — don't lock up money you'll need soon.
  • Revisit the goal every few months and increase contributions as your income grows.
