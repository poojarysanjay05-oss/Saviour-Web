How this is calculated

We multiply your average meal cost by how often you eat out, then estimate home-cooked meals at roughly 30% of that cost to show potential savings.

Monthly Cost = Cost per Meal × Meals/Week × 4.33

Example: ₹350 per meal, 6 times a week, comes to roughly ₹9,100 a month — over ₹1,00,000 a year.

Tips to cut dining out costs

  • Meal-prep on weekends so a home-cooked option is always ready.
  • Set a weekly cap on dining-out spend rather than counting individual meals.
  • Order in bulk or share with a friend to lower delivery fees per person.
  • Save dining out for occasions that feel worth it, not daily convenience.
