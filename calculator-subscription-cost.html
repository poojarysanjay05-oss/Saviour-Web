How this is calculated

This tool simply adds up every subscription you enter and multiplies the monthly total by 12, so you see the true annual cost of things that feel small day to day.

Yearly Cost = (Streaming + Music + Cloud + Other) × 12

Example: ₹500/month in streaming, ₹150 in music and ₹200 in cloud storage adds up to ₹10,200 a year.

Tips to cut subscription costs

  • Cancel anything you haven't opened in the last 30 days.
  • Switch to annual billing for services you'll definitely keep using.
  • Rotate streaming services instead of subscribing to all of them at once.
  • Use a family or group plan to split the cost with people you trust.
