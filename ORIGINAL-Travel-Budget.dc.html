How this is calculated

We multiply your daily on-ground budget by trip length, add flights, then include a 10% buffer for the inevitable extras that come up while travelling.

Total = (Daily Budget × Days + Flights) × 1.1

Example: a 5-day trip at ₹3,000/day with ₹12,000 flights comes to roughly ₹29,700 with buffer.

Tips to travel on budget

  • Book flights and stays 6-8 weeks ahead for the best prices.
  • Set a daily spending cap and track it as you go, not just before the trip.
  • Use local transport and street food over taxis and hotel restaurants.
  • Start a dedicated savings pot months ahead so the trip doesn't strain your budget.
