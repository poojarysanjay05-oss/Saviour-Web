How this is calculated

This is a straightforward division of your total trip cost across the months you have left to save, giving you a clear monthly target.

Monthly Saving = Trip Cost / Months Remaining

Example: a ₹60,000 trip planned 6 months out needs ₹10,000 saved each month.

Tips to hit your target

  • Automate the monthly amount right after payday so it's never optional.
  • Book flights and hotels early to lock in prices before they rise.
  • Put any bonus or cashback directly toward the trip fund.
  • Track the trip fund separately so it's never mixed with everyday spending.
