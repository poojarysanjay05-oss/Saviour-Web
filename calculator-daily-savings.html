How this is calculated

Your daily saving is converted to a monthly amount and grown with compound interest, month over month, for the full period you choose.

Monthly Saving = Daily × 30.4, grown monthly at your expected return

Example: saving ₹100 a day at 7% for 5 years grows to roughly ₹2,20,000 — about ₹37,000 of that from growth alone.

Tips to make it stick

  • Round up everyday purchases and save the difference automatically.
  • Start small — even ₹50/day builds a habit you can grow later.
  • Move it to a separate account so it's out of sight, out of mind.
  • Review your progress monthly to stay motivated.
